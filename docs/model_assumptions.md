This model has not been validated, but merely an experimental simulation designed for learning Python for engineering. Errors may be present, and assumptions have been made, as may be seen below.

- **Storage:** total containment is represented by one rigid vessel with a fixed mass of ideal air at uniform pressure and temperature. Water is assumed incompressible and leakage, real-gas effects, pipe losses, wall thermal mass and other related sources of error are omitted in the scope of this simulation.
- **Heat transfer:** seawater stays at 14°C and 1,025 kg/m³. The heat-transfer coefficient is 10 W/(m²·K); effective area is 500 m² at 4,080 m³ and scales with volume to the power 2/3. Air properties are constant: R = 287.05 J/(kg·K), cv = 718 J/(kg·K).
- **Initial conditions:** every run starts at 85 bar absolute and 14°C. At the default vessel size, gas volume starts at 3,000 m³, with limits of 1,200–3,900 m³. These volumes scale with total containment. Initial stored energy can contribute to discharge.
- **Equipment:** pump and turbine efficiencies are fixed at 82% and 92%. The selected electrical rating applies in both directions. Pressure limits are 80–200 bar gauge (81.01325–201.01325 bar absolute). Depth sets external seawater pressure.
- **Design bounds:** slider ranges are only modelling limits and validated limits. Containment volume may represent a vessel bundle. Deployment depth is a modelling input that can be changed and not the actual bathymetry at the wind-data point.
- **Wind:** hourly ERA5 100 m wind for 2025 represents the reference point near Malta, not site measurements. The idealised turbine curve uses 3/11/25 m/s cut-in/rated/cut-out speeds. Wakes, turbulence, density variation and other sources of error are omitted in the scope of this simulation.
- **Control and numerics:** hourly wind is held constant during 60-second Euler steps. The target averages the current and preceding hourly samples; startup uses only available history. Commands follow power and predicted state limits, but passive thermal relaxation can still cause outside the allowed range, and no active pressure regulation has been modelled.
- **Results:** This simulation assumes the grid can accept output above the smoothing target. Average discharge power is discharged electrical energy divided by the full selected duration, including idle and charging periods. For runs longer than 31 days, charts show hourly average power and end-of-hour storage states, while calculations and downloads retain one-minute resolution.
