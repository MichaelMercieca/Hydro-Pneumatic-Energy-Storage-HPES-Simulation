This lumped-parameter model supports comparative studies; it is not a validated equipment design.

- **Storage:** total containment is represented by one rigid vessel with a fixed mass of ideal air at uniform pressure and temperature. Water is incompressible. Leakage, real-gas effects, pipe losses, wall thermal mass and structural behaviour are omitted.
- **Heat transfer:** seawater stays at 14°C and 1,025 kg/m³. The heat-transfer coefficient is 10 W/(m²·K); effective area is 500 m² at 4,080 m³ and scales with volume to the power 2/3. Air properties are constant: R = 287.05 J/(kg·K), cv = 718 J/(kg·K).
- **Initial conditions:** every run starts at 85 bar absolute and 14°C. At the default vessel size, gas volume starts at 3,000 m³, with limits of 1,200–3,900 m³. These volumes scale with total containment. Initial stored energy can contribute to discharge.
- **Equipment:** pump and turbine efficiencies are fixed at 82% and 92%. The selected electrical rating applies in both directions. Pressure limits are 80–200 bar gauge (81.01325–201.01325 bar absolute). Depth sets external seawater pressure.
- **Design bounds:** slider ranges are modelling limits, not certified equipment limits. Containment volume may represent a vessel bundle. Deployment depth is a scenario input, not measured bathymetry at the wind-data point.
- **Wind:** hourly ERA5 100 m wind for 2025 represents the reference point near Malta, not site measurements. The idealised turbine curve uses 3/11/25 m/s cut-in/rated/cut-out speeds. Wakes, turbulence, density variation and availability losses are omitted.
- **Control and numerics:** hourly wind is held constant during 60-second Euler steps. The target averages the current and preceding hourly samples; startup uses only available history. Commands respect power and predicted state limits, but passive thermal relaxation can still cause pressure excursions, which are flagged. No active pressure regulation is modelled.
- **Results:** unabsorbed surplus remains exported above target. Average discharge power is discharged electrical energy divided by the entire selected duration, including idle and charging periods. It is neither storage capacity nor round-trip efficiency. Long-period charts use hourly summaries; calculations and downloads retain one-minute resolution.
